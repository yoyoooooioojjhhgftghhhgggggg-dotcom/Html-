Introduction

Electonic Commerce is the process of buying and selling products and services through the Internt. It makes shopping easier and faster for people around the world.

Types of E-COMMerce

B2C

Business to COnsumer: Businesses sell products directly to customers

B2B

Business TO Business: companies sell products to other companies.

C2C

Consumer to Consumer: People sell products to other people.

C2B

Consumer to Business: Individuals provide services to companies

Advantages of E-COMMerce

  • Shopping is available 24 hours.
  • It saves time and effort.
  • Customers can compare prices.
  • Businesses reach more customers.

Disadvantages of

  • Security and privacy risks.
  • Delivery may take time.
  • Customers cannot touch products.
  • SOMe websites may be unreliable.
